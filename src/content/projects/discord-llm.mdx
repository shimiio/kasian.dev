## no reason at all

**There was no plan behind bob: one day I just wanted my own Discord bot.** So I made one. He runs on a language model on my laptop through Ollama, with no API keys and nothing paid. His character lives in a plain text file: write a few lines about who he is, and that is who shows up in the chat.

## not smart, but funny

**bob isn't very capable: he reads the channel and answers, and that's about it.** He waits a few seconds while people type, then answers everyone in one message, and he knows who said what. That alone makes a chat a lot louder.

## "stop being toxic, bro"

**His temper is all over the place.** He often gets heated himself, yet when someone calls him names or the chat turns into a round of insults, he's the one who steps in with something like: "Stop being toxic, bro. Tell me about music instead." Whether that comes from the model or the prompt, I'm not sure. Probably both.

He still needs work: a better model and a calmer prompt. But as a joke, he does his job.

*bob, as in боб: a bean. Hence the sunglasses. And honestly, I call everything bob: even the pug plush at home started out as Bobby.*
